About Tasha

Tasha Star is a fitness and lifestyle coach with more than 15 years of experience in the health and fitness industry. With a background spanning psychotherapy, biomechanics, strength training and physique development, she takes a comprehensive approach that considers far more than exercise alone.

Tasha works with both men and women who are navigating hormonal changes, health concerns, changes in body composition, declining strength, low energy and reduced confidence in their bodies.

Her approach bridges the gap between clinical recommendations and real-life implementation. She creates personalized strategies around strength training, movement, recovery, sleep, stress management and sustainable lifestyle habits — all while working within the recommendations of the patient’s clinical care team.

Her goal is not simply to change how patients look, but to help them become stronger, healthier, more confident and better equipped to maintain their physical health, independence and quality of life long term.

Who she works best with

Best-fit patients

These services are appropriate for men and women who are experiencing:

  • Hormonal changes or imbalances
  • Sexual health concerns that may be affected by physical health, stress, confidence or lifestyle
  • Unexplained changes in weight or body composition
  • Loss of muscle, strength or physical confidence
  • Low energy, poor recovery or exercise intolerance
  • Difficulty knowing how to exercise safely and effectively
  • Inconsistent habits despite understanding what they “should” be doing
  • Stress, poor sleep or a demanding lifestyle affecting their health
  • A need for accountability and practical implementation support
  • Frustration that previous fitness strategies are no longer working
  • A desire to age proactively while maintaining strength, independence and quality of life
  • A need to rebuild confidence and feel stronger and more capable in their body

Her services are also a strong fit for patients who have received clinical recommendations but need individualized fitness programming, lifestyle strategies, structure and accountability to translate those recommendations into their everyday lives.

Scope

What Tasha covers

Her role within the clinic is focused specifically on fitness, movement, recovery, stress management, sleep habits, behavioural change and lifestyle implementation.

Within that role she may discuss general nutrition habits and supplements where appropriate and within her training and competence, particularly where they relate to fitness, exercise, recovery and general healthy lifestyle behaviours.

Tasha is not a Registered Dietitian. She does not provide Registered Dietitian nutrition assessment, medical nutrition therapy, treatment of medical conditions through diet, therapeutic or prescriptive meal planning, or any service represented as dietetic care. Those remain under the direction of the clinic’s qualified nutrition and medical professionals.

Looking for individualized nutrition support?

Patients seeking individualized dietary assessment, medical nutrition therapy or other dietitian services can book with a Revitalize Registered Dietitian. Registered Dietitian services may be eligible for reimbursement under some extended-health plans, depending on your individual coverage.

Through Revitalize

The Fitness & Lifestyle Assessment

The Fitness & Lifestyle Assessment is a comprehensive 45-minute virtual consultation designed to evaluate the patient’s current fitness, physical health, lifestyle and behavioural patterns.

Before the appointment, the patient completes a detailed intake questionnaire covering areas such as medical and injury history, current symptoms, movement and exercise, sleep, stress, energy, recovery, daily routines, previous fitness experience, barriers and personal goals.

When available, relevant notes and recommendations from the clinic’s healthcare practitioners are also reviewed before the consultation. This allows the assessment to complement the patient’s clinical care and ensures that all fitness and lifestyle recommendations are safe, appropriate and aligned with their overall treatment plan.

During the consultation, we explore:

  • Current movement, activity and exercise habits
  • Strength, mobility and physical limitations
  • Exercise history and experience
  • Sleep quality and recovery
  • Stress levels and nervous-system load
  • Energy, motivation and consistency
  • Lifestyle demands and time constraints
  • Previous approaches and why they may not have worked
  • Current goals, priorities and readiness for change

The purpose of the assessment is to identify the factors that may be preventing the patient from progressing and determine the most appropriate next steps. Following the assessment, the patient receives clear, personalized fitness and lifestyle recommendations and, when appropriate, can continue into a more comprehensive programming or coaching option.

What this appointment is, and what it is not

The assessment and follow-ups are focused consultations. They give you direction, education and a clear sense of what to work on. They do not include the written, customized training programme that comes with Tasha’s Action Plan or coaching options.

Optional

Want more structure and ongoing support?

Not everyone needs or wants a coaching program. Some patients simply want an assessment, clear recommendations and the occasional follow-up, and that is a perfectly good way to work.

Others want more detailed direction, individualized programming, accountability and ongoing coaching. Tasha offers that through her own business, Star Lifestyle. These are her independent services — booked, managed and paid for directly with her, not through Revitalize.

Action Plan

$699

The Action Plan is designed for patients who need professional direction and a personalized fitness and lifestyle strategy but may not require ongoing high-touch coaching.

It includes an in-depth review of the patient’s assessment, medical considerations, goals, limitations and current lifestyle, followed by a customized plan. Depending on the patient’s needs, the plan may include:

  • Personalized strength training
  • Cardiovascular exercise recommendations
  • Mobility and movement work
  • Recovery strategies
  • Sleep-supportive habits
  • Stress-management practices
  • Daily activity goals
  • Realistic lifestyle routines

This option is best suited to patients who are relatively self-directed but need clarity, structure and a plan that accounts for their health, hormones, physical abilities and lifestyle.

Three-Month Coaching

$1,699

The three-month coaching program provides a more comprehensive level of support for patients who need help implementing changes consistently and adjusting their plan as their body, symptoms and lifestyle evolve. The program may include:

  • Personalized fitness and lifestyle programming
  • Strength, mobility and cardiovascular recommendations
  • Recovery and stress-management strategies
  • Sleep and daily-routine support
  • Ongoing progress monitoring
  • Regular scheduled monthly assessments and check-ins
  • Program adjustments based on progress, symptoms and clinical recommendations
  • Messaging and voice-note support between check-ins (Monday to Friday)
  • Accountability, education and behavioural coaching
  • Collaboration with the patient’s healthcare team when appropriate

This option allows enough time to move beyond simply creating a plan and begin building the skills, consistency and confidence required for sustainable results.

Six-Month Coaching

$3,199

The six-month coaching program offers the highest level of long-term support. It is intended for patients with more complex concerns, multiple competing goals or a longer history of inconsistent exercise, hormonal challenges, chronic stress, low energy or difficulty maintaining results.

The extended timeframe allows us to work through progressive phases of assessment, implementation, adjustment and maintenance. Rather than relying on an aggressive short-term approach, patients are supported in building strength, improving body composition, increasing energy, improving physical confidence and establishing sustainable routines that can evolve with their health and lifestyle.

Ongoing support is primarily provided through scheduled check-ins, messaging and voice notes. Additional phone or virtual follow-up appointments may also be arranged when needed.

How care works

Two routes, and who handles each

Through Revitalize. The Fitness & Lifestyle Assessment and individual follow-ups are booked and paid through Revitalize, and can be covered by your Thrive Allied Health Benefit. Tasha coordinates with your Revitalize care team where appropriate. You can continue at this level indefinitely — there is no requirement to move on to anything else.

Directly with Star Lifestyle. The Action Plan and the coaching programs are Tasha’s own services. She sets her own pricing, scheduling and terms, and you book and pay with her directly. Revitalize does not bill for them.

Unleash Synergy

Relationship, Sex & Couples Therapy · Independent Practice

Why they are here

An independent practice in our network

Some of what affects sexual function, intimacy and connection is physical. Some of it is not. Where the psychological, emotional or relational side is what needs attention, we may suggest Unleash Synergy.

Unleash Synergy is an independent practice, not part of Revitalize Your Health. They set their own fees, keep their own clinical records and provide their own care. We suggest them because we think highly of their work, and because their way of talking with people about sexual health closely matches our own.

In their own words

When relationships, sex, or connection feel hard

You might be thoughtful and self-aware, yet still find yourself stuck in familiar patterns. Insight alone doesn’t always lead to lasting relief — and if you’re feeling disconnected, you don’t have to navigate it alone.

Unleash Synergy is a specialized psychotherapy practice in Toronto and across Ontario. They help individuals and couples work through emotional, sexual, and relational blocks so you can create a life filled with love, deep connection, and passion.

How they can help you

Relational & Couples Growth

Break free from recurring arguments, close the emotional or physical distance with your partner, navigate infidelity, or move smoothly through major life shifts like dating, marriage, or separation.

Sex & Intimacy Therapy

Talk openly about desire differences, performance or arousal concerns, intimacy anxiety, or explore non-monogamy and kink-friendly relationship spaces without judgment.

Process grief, release shame or stigma rooted in religious or conservative upbringings, and rebuild confidence in who you are.

Their approach

A safe, inclusive space to thrive

Their accredited therapists combine evidence-based clinical therapy with a warm, non-judgmental approach. Whether you prefer in-person sessions in Toronto or virtual appointments anywhere in Ontario, they tailor care to your unique journey.

In their own words: “Most of our services are eligible for coverage through extended health insurance plans.” Coverage depends on your individual plan, and it is worth confirming with Unleash Synergy and your insurer before you begin.

Why we work together

Two sides of the same concern

Sexual concerns often have both a physical and a psychological side, and addressing only one is a common reason people stall. We assess and treat the medical and physiological contributors. Unleash Synergy works with the relational, emotional and psychological ones.

Referrals run both ways, based on what a person actually needs. There is no financial arrangement between the two practices. If we suggest them, it is because we think it would help.

Not sure which side of this you need?

If you are unsure whether what you are experiencing is physical, psychological or both, raise it at your appointment. We will be honest about which of us is better placed to help.
